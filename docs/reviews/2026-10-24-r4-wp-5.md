### q=0 boundary determination

**The no-quote q=0 case is an input/provenance validation boundary, not a remaining must-fix
contract contradiction.** This does not certify a parsed cash-only result as a genuine run.

The immutable contract requires the largest affordable q, says to preserve cash and record
the reason when q is zero, and prohibits retry while the target is unchanged (contract
lines 112-119). Those requirements still apply to execution. Production preparation retains
the approved bars, checks all sessions and extracts every needed raw open
(`input.rs:49-72,145-185,241-264`). The unchanged engine computes q from that open before
emitting ZERO_SHARE_QUANTITY (`ledger.rs:295-309,395-409`); it does not infer q from an empty
result record.

P0-12 commits the external input through five pins and the bars byte hash, and requires the
ledger's quantities, cash, costs, NAV and unfilled reasons (contract lines 185-192). It does
not require a raw quote in an empty, zero-position record, nor require a result parser to
recover the bars from their hash. Consistency of available result evidence is checked here;
authenticity against the external artifact remains a separate check. Treating the hash as
proof of unavailable bytes would overclaim, just as treating EngineIdentity syntax as
binary attestation would.

The new helper therefore rejects q=0 when either ledger supplies the same date/instrument
opening quote and one share plus fee is affordable (`contract.rs:2090-2129`). If neither
supplies that quote, `2117-2120` preserves the result without inventing a price. Rejecting
all such results would also reject legitimate expensive-share/no-trade cases allowed by
P0-06; adding a mandatory quote or another wire field is not needed to close F4's positive
underfill defect. The parser must not be described as independently proving q=0 affordability
in this no-quote case. Actual artifact replay remains necessary for that claim.
